
# 🐾 Pixel Pet — Your Tiny Digital Bestie

> A tiny virtual pet, a little bit of pixel magic, and a whole lot of love. 💗

Welcome to **Pixel Pet**, a cute browser-based virtual pet game where you take care of your own digital companion, Mochi! Feed your pet, play together, and make sure they get enough rest.

Built using simple web technologies, Pixel Pet is a fun beginner-friendly project that brings a little joy to your everyday coding.

## ✨ Features

- 🐱 **Virtual Pet:** Meet Mochi, your adorable digital companion.
- 🍎 **Feed Your Pet:** Keep Mochi happy with tasty treats.
- 🎾 **Playtime:** Play with Mochi to boost happiness and earn points.
- 🌙 **Sleep Mode:** Let Mochi rest and restore energy.
- 📊 **Live Stats:** Track hunger, happiness, and energy.
- ⭐ **Care Points:** Earn points by looking after your pet.
- 🎨 **Cute UI:** Pastel colors, playful animations, and a cozy design.
- 🔄 **Reset Game:** Start a fresh adventure whenever you want.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Play on desktop or mobile browsers.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Open Pixel Pet in your browser.
2. Meet Mochi!
3. Click **FEED** to satisfy hunger.
4. Click **PLAY** to increase happiness.
5. Click **SLEEP** to restore energy.
6. Earn care points and keep your pet in a good mood!

Remember: even a tiny digital pet needs a little love. ♡

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — Game structure
- **CSS3** — Styling, animations, and responsive layout
- **JavaScript** — Game logic, interactions, and pet stats
- **Google Fonts** — Retro pixel typography

No frameworks, no backend, and no installation required.
